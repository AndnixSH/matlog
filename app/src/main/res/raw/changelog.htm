Changelog

3.0.0
  • Rebuilt for Android 17 (API 37); Android 6.0 and later
  • Port Android Studio's key-value search: tag:, message:, package:, process:, line:, level:, is:, age:, name: and pid:, with &, | and brackets. The queries mean what they do in Studio. Key terms are tinted in the search box, and completed as you type
  • Add "Find in log" (Ctrl+F), which marks and steps through every occurrence instead of hiding the lines that do not match
  • Offer tag names only after "tag:", not for bare text
  • Read logs through Shizuku when there is no root, in the order root > Shizuku > READ_LOGS, so Android's "access all device logs" prompt stays away on most devices
  • Save logs without a folder picker again: /sdcard/matlog up to Android 10, Documents/matlog on Android 11 and later, and /sdcard/matlog with root in the F-Droid build. Settings > Log folder keeps them somewhere else
  • Add an Auto app theme that follows the system's light/dark setting, and start a fresh install with the colour scheme that matches it
  • Load the log without reading the whole buffer twice: 18 s down to about 1 s on a device with a large log
  • Flatten the bottom bar to the screen edge, seat the record button in its cradle, stop the bar clipping its icons, and hide the two together on scroll
  • Let the log list show through behind the record and about dialogs, and stop the dark dialogs washing out against it
  • Fix the crash when a recording starts, and the recorder hanging at "Loading recorder"
  • Fix a finished recording opening with live lines piling up on top of it
  • Fix root log reads hanging on Android 11, and give up on an unanswered su after 20 s instead of waiting for ever
  • Fix every save creating another "matlog (1)", "matlog (2)" folder
  • Fix the freeze when opening the list of saved logs
  • Fix the colour scheme reverting whenever the app theme was applied
  • Fix the home-screen widget's fixed size and floating record badge
